I am a Professor in the Computer Engineering Department at the Izmir Institute of Technology (IZTECH), where I chaired the department from 2021 to 2024 and currently serve as an elected member of the university senate. Before joining IZTECH in 2020, I spent more than two decades in the Department of Electrical and Electronics Engineering at Middle East Technical University (METU), as Assistant and then Associate Professor.

My work sits at the meeting point of computer networks, computer architecture and combinatorial optimization. Over the years this has meant designing high-speed packet processing engines on FPGAs, studying application mapping for networks-on-chip, timing analysis for real-time embedded software, and algorithms for spanning-tree and concave-cost network design problems. The IP lookup architecture developed with Oğuzhan Erdem became a triadic patent — European, US and Japanese — under the METU Technopark portfolio.

I received my PhD from the Department of Computation at the University of Manchester Institute of Science and Technology (UMIST) in 1996, and both my MSc and BSc in Electrical and Electronics Engineering from METU. I have supervised 3 PhD and more than 40 MSc theses; my former students now work in academia and industry across Europe, the US and Turkey.

Research interests

  • Communication and computer networks — synthesis and design, architectures and protocols, fast packet switching and processing systems
  • Computer systems architecture and hardware — microprocessor systems and applications, reconfigurable systems, embedded systems
  • Optimization, graph algorithms and applications — optimization theory and mathematical programming, algorithms and advanced data structures
