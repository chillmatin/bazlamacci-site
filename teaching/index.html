Laboratory development and supervision

Microcomputer Systems Laboratory

The Department of Electrical and Electronics Engineering at METU used MC6800-based microcomputer devices and training kits for microprocessor and computer architecture courses before 2000. After securing appropriate funding, these laboratories were modernised in 2001 with new laptop computers, Motorola (later Freescale) 6811 based training sets and supporting equipment. A completely new set of lecture notes and laboratory manuals was prepared, and the laboratories were managed and supervised thereafter. Starting in 2016, the associated courses and laboratories were modernised again, re-organised and enhanced using ARM and FPGA based training sets.

Computer Systems and Networks Research Laboratory

Established in the Electrical and Electronics Engineering Department in 2002 as a graduate research laboratory, equipped with personal computers, workstations, a variety of advanced microcomputer development environments, DSP cards and network simulation software. Many undergraduate and graduate students have participated in its research activities.
