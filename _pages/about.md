I am a Professor in the [Computer Engineering Department](http://ceng.iyte.edu.tr/) at the [Izmir Institute of Technology (IZTECH)](https://en.iyte.edu.tr/). Before joining IZTECH in 2020, I spent more than two decades in the Department of Electrical and Electronics Engineering at [Middle East Technical University (METU)](http://www.metu.edu.tr/), where I served as Assistant and then Associate Professor.

My work sits at the meeting point of **computer networks**, **computer architecture** and **combinatorial optimization**. Over the years this has meant designing high-speed packet processing engines on FPGAs, studying application mapping for networks-on-chip, and developing algorithms for spanning-tree and concave-cost network design problems. The IP lookup architecture developed with Oğuzhan Erdem became a triadic patent (European, US and Japanese) under the METU Technopolis portfolio.

I received my PhD in Computing from the University of Manchester Institute of Science and Technology ([UMIST](https://www.manchester.ac.uk/discover/history-heritage/history/umist/)) in 1996, and both my MSc and BSc in Electrical and Electronics Engineering from METU.

Research results organised by subject area are collected on the [research]({{ '/research/' | relative_url }}) page; a chronological list of papers, patents and theses is on the [publications]({{ '/publications/' | relative_url }}) page.

#### Research interests

- **Communication and computer networks** — synthesis and design, architectures and protocols, fast packet switching and processing systems
- **Computer systems architecture and hardware** — microprocessor systems and applications, embedded systems, reconfigurable systems, FPGA/SoC based systems and applications
- **Graph algorithms and applications** — optimization theory and mathematical programming, algorithms and advanced data structures
